# NERVYX ONE Brand Kit

**Descriptor:** Adaptive Market Intelligence  
**Primary tagline:** Sense. Decide. Adapt.  
**Secondary line:** One system. Every market state.

## Brand idea
NERVYX ONE is presented as a market nervous system: many data senses, parallel intelligence, a single decision layer, and defensive reflexes that fail closed.

## Logo concept
The symbol combines an angular **N** with a crossing signal stroke that reveals an **X**. Four nodes represent ingestion, inference, validation, and execution. The gradient moves from neural violet to decision cyan.

## Product family
- **NERVYX SENSE** - data ingestion and market-state trust
- **NERVYX CORE** - PPO + MASA inference
- **NERVYX SHIFT** - regime and strategy routing
- **NERVYX GUARD** - risk and trust gates
- **NERVYX REPLAY** - decision reconstruction
- **NERVYX EXECUTE** - paper/live order lifecycle
- **NERVYX OBSERVE** - monitoring and operational proof

## Primary theme
**Midnight Neural** is the default interface. Use violet/blue for intelligence, cyan for active signals, mint for verified/clear states, amber for caution, and coral only for risk or failure.

## Typography
- Display: Inter Display ExtraBold/Bold
- Interface: Inter Regular/Medium/SemiBold
- Data: a clean monospace such as DejaVu Sans Mono

## Voice
Calm, exact, evidence-first, and controlled. Avoid hype, guaranteed-return language, casino imagery, rockets, coins, bulls/bears, or aggressive red/green overload.

## Usage
- Maintain clear space around the logo equal to the width of the symbol's crossing stroke.
- Do not distort, rotate, recolor individual symbol strokes, add glow-heavy effects, or place the full-color mark on noisy imagery.
- On dark backgrounds, use the dark lockup; on light backgrounds, use the light lockup.
