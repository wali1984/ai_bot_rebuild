### RE-001 — risk `DENY` is not authoritative for ordinary paper admission

- **Evidence:** source-proven and observed in Redis.
- **Source:** `v2/backend/app/cli/v2_orchestrator_arbitration_loop.py:744`; `v2/backend/app/cli/v2_risk_gateway_live_loop.py:567`; `v2/backend/app/cli/v2_trade_management_paper_loop.py:16738`, `:17167`, `:21938`, `:28720`, `:29193`; `v2/backend/app/services/paper_exploration/policy.py:1434`.
- **Mechanism:** the orchestrator creates a provisional risk ID and sets its paper flag before gateway evaluation. The paper loop dereferences and records the real `DENY`, but its ordinary A+ admission tests whether a risk ID exists plus whether local pre-trade passed; it does not require the action to be allow. Exploration policy does require allow, so the two admission paths disagree.
- **Impact:** risk lineage can look complete while the risk decision does not control a paper fill. Paper outcomes, position history, and trainer feedback can therefore be produced from risk-denied entries.
- **Required proof before closure:** one canonical risk-decision contract, an allow-action assertion at the sole fill-write boundary, negative tests for `DENY`, missing/stale/mismatched IDs, and end-to-end evidence that a denied candidate cannot materialize a fill.
- **2026-07-17 mitigation:** routeable orchestrator signals now use the gateway-compatible `rd_dec_<prediction>` ID and remain `RISK_PENDING`; `_paper_policy_intent_decision_dereference` no longer manufactures inline PASS; ordinary A+ requires an exact resolved `ALLOW`. Paper-only `open_*`/`proceed_*` aliases can satisfy only the orchestrator stage and never substitute for canonical risk. The trainer now marks every decision preview/handoff `TRAINER_NON_AUTHORITATIVE_PROPOSAL` and no longer writes canonical per-ID decisions/indexes; the paper loop observes but does not backfill missing records. Runtime showed the corrected risk-pending signal shape. This finding remains open because the live-oriented gateway intentionally denies while disarmed, paper exploration is a separate non-A+ plane, and canonical per-ID orchestrator production is not yet source-proven.

### RE-063 — P0 trainer lifecycle is source-integrated but has no controlled runtime proof

### RE-064 — P1 trainer semantics are helper-mitigated; lifecycle durability, cost authority and adaptivity remain open

### RE-065 — partial-close economics now survive source-level restart reconstruction; stored runtime history is unproved

- **Former defect/blast radius:** rebuilding an open position from fills could lose already allocated entry fee/slippage basis, replay a consumed generation or net a historical fill without a quantity-conservation receipt. A restart after partial closes could therefore change later net PnL, confidence labels, PPO reward, margin/equity and promotion evidence.
- **Current source contract:** `PAPER_OPEN_POSITION_RECONSTRUCTION_V1` plus canonical SHA-256 binds position/generation/version, aware ordered entry/open/reconstruction clocks, symbol/side, net quantity, average entry, ordered source-fill IDs, realized PnL and incurred/remaining/allocated entry fee/slippage ledgers with materialized fallback rates/sources/status and paper OPEN_POSITION safety. A valid snapshot seeds state before fill replay and must round-trip exactly. Legacy partial, tampered, future-clock, incomplete-ledger or mixed complete/incomplete same-side basis fails closed.
- **Generation/netting proof:** a close suppresses a whole generation only when explicit final-close and pre-close=closed or remaining=0 prove full consumption. Each historical netting fill requires a versioned hash receipt bound to close ID, position generation, fill ID, side and `input=consumed+residual`. Accepted-fill disk compaction retains both contracts; fallback entry cost is materialized once and conserved across restarts.
- **Validation/runtime boundary:** the full paper-management plus new CLI persistence lane passed 509 cases; focused adversarial/persistence passed 16 and the existing compaction/rehydration selector passed 4 with 534 deselected. Scoped Ruff and compile passed; the full CLI Ruff inventory still has 112 unrelated pre-existing findings. No paper service restart, existing-state migration or deployed rehydration occurred.
- **Required proof before closure:** read-only inventory of persisted open/partial generations; quarantine/migration plan for legacy rows; controlled restart proving identical pre/post hashes, quantities, cost ledgers, margin/equity and next-close economics; invalid/tampered runtime sample rejection; then repeated compaction/restart observation without duplicate or lost fills.

### RE-066 — durable behavior-receipt archive is source-integrated through outcome, but trainer-consumption closure and bounded retention are missing

- **Current source contract:** `durable_behavior_receipt_archive.py` uses blob schema `v2_durable_behavior_receipt_archive_v1`, event schema `v2_behavior_receipt_lifecycle_event_v1` and default root `.local_data/v2_native_trainer/durable_behavior_receipt_archive`. Receipt blobs and per-receipt events are canonical-SHA addressed, atomically create-or-identical, fsynced and read back. A per-receipt `fcntl.flock` encloses lifecycle read/check/write/readback; duplicate stored event types are corruption and fail closed. Required event order is `PUBLISHED → ENTRY_ACCEPTED → OUTCOME_FINALIZED → TRAINER_CONSUMED`; every event is paper-only/no-live/no-order and the final event requires a 64-hex PPO consumption update key.
- **Integrated boundary:** publisher archive+`PUBLISHED` precedes immutable no-expiry Redis receipt and exact route eligibility. Paper entry re-verifies archive/hash/published state and exact configured fee SHA before appending `ENTRY_ACCEPTED`. Feedback re-verifies entry event/fee binding and appends `OUTCOME_FINALIZED`, exposing archive verified/finalized and retention-required fields. Missing/legacy/conflicting archive state cannot qualify as exact PPO; a corrupt sampled row cannot be relabeled as supervised.
- **Open P0:** no trainer call appends `TRAINER_CONSUMED` after `v2_exact_ppo_consumption_ledger_v3` records the matching durable disposition. `receipt_lifecycle_status.retention_required` therefore remains true. The archive has no garbage collector, so physical blobs are currently never deleted; this prevents premature deletion but creates unbounded disk growth. Canonical hashes are tamper-evident/self-authenticating, not keyed authentication, off-host backup or restore evidence.
- **Related checkpoint retention:** `checkpoint_retention_status` now emits `native_cuda_trainer_checkpoint_retention_manifest_v2`, scans serving/candidate/rejected stores and pins active serving, latest candidate, artifacts intersecting pending SQLite claims and SQLite/WAL/SHM. - **Required proof before closure:** append `TRAINER_CONSUMED` only after successful matching ledger disposition; make retry/crash/reconcile idempotent and impossible before the ledger commit; test receipt/update-key mismatch and event conflict; define a versioned retention/backup/restore policy that never removes `retention_required=true`; and prove bounded disk growth in a held-service burn-in before enabling timers.

## Closure rules

A finding is not closed by a code comment, status JSON, or a passing happy-path unit test. Closure requires:

1. the exact source change and impact analysis;
2. isolated negative/edge tests;
3. point-in-time and dirty-sample proofs where data is involved;
4. deployed-unit reconciliation where operations are involved;
5. regenerated atlas and contract registries;
6. a read-only runtime verification snapshot;
7. explicit operator approval before any live-execution, order, risk, strategy, PPO, MASA, or destructive-retention change.
