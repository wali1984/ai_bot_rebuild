- Production paths inspected: trainer dataset builder/data loader, RL observation builder, MASA adapter, orchestrator arbitration loop, paper trade management loop, paper execution worker, risk gateway live loop, replay debugger, market-state integrity services.

## Immediate safety-critical gaps

1. No shared production `MarketStateEnvelope` contract spans trainer, RL inference, risk, paper execution, and live-intent generation.
2. No production `EventTimeAligner` exists; point-in-time alignment is enforced only partially by row validators and fully only inside the synthetic harness.
3. Trainer dataset builder and hybrid trainer data loader do not hard-stop on the trust filter.
4. Real RL observation and MASA adapter contracts do not carry the required cutoff/hash/timestamp metadata.
5. The live risk gateway is still too thin to be the final data-trust authority for live-intent generation.
6. Replay is enriched in places, but there is no guaranteed persisted replay artifact for every approved and blocked real-path decision.
