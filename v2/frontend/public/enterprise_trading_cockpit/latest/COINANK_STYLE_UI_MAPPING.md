# CoinAnk-Style UI Mapping

The cockpit uses CoinAnk-style density without copying branding:

- Market overview cards: OI change, funding, liquidation risk, quarantine alerts.
- Derivatives table: price, 1H/24H change, funding, OI, long/short, risk state.
- Chart panel: candlestick and volume fixture chart with signal/risk markers.
- Risk widgets: risk blocks and 2X quarantine state.
- Exchange cards: Binance USD-M, KuCoin, and MEXC read-only linking design.

All market data is labeled `STATIC_PROOF_FIXTURE` until read-only market feeds are wired.
