# 17 100x–1000x Goal Alignment

## Requirement ID
V2-GROWTH-SAFETY-ALIGNMENT-001

## Mission statement
Aggressive growth (100x–1000x objective) remains the mission, but must be executed through fast and safe iteration with hard safety controls.

## Mandatory safety conditions
1. No compounding without expectancy proof
- Compounding logic remains blocked unless expectancy is demonstrated by validated data.

2. No 90% winrate acceptance without tail-loss review
- Any high-winrate claim must include drawdown/tail-loss stress evidence before promotion.

3. Risk Gateway mandatory
- Risk Gateway remains final trade authority; bypass is non-compliant.

4. Replay/paper validation mandatory
- Strategy/profile changes must pass replay/paper criteria before live candidate status.

5. Live trading blocked by default
- Default state is live-disabled until explicit readiness GO + approvals.

6. Human approval for dangerous changes
- High-impact changes require human sign-off and immutable audit record.

## Fast-but-safe iteration model
- Iterate quickly using:
  - replay and paper environments
  - explainability and attribution visibility
  - controlled policy rollout with rollback capability
- Promote only changes with evidence-backed safety and expectancy outcomes.

## Platform design alignment
- Enterprise website enables fast diagnosis and controlled action.
- Hot-reload pipeline enables speed without full restart risk.
- Multi-trader/multi-exchange expansion is gated by readiness and risk controls.

## Pre-architecture acceptance
- Growth objective explicitly preserved.
- Safety conditions are mandatory constraints, not optional guidelines.
