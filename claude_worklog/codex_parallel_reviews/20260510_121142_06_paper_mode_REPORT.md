## Verdict

BLOCKED.

The paper-mode flag itself is correctly narrow and non-live, and the inspected MVP surfaces do not contain live execution code. However, Paper Mode MVP readiness is not enforceable end to end because the risk gateway and paper ledger synthesize `live_blocked=True` internally instead of requiring the composed `PaperModeFlag` as an input. As a result, missing, invalid, stale, or mismatched paper-mode evidence cannot block risk approval or paper-ledger recording.

### Paper ledger integration

BLOCKED.

`v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:26-30` accepts only `decision: RiskDecisionRecord` and `now_ms_clock`. It does not require a `PaperModeFlag`. The returned ledger entry at `v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:80-92` copies risk lineage fields and hard-codes `live_blocked=True`, but it does not preserve `paper_mode`, `paper_mode_flag_emitted_ts_ms`, or the flag object used to authorize the paper path.

The domain record mirrors that gap: `v2/backend/app/domain/paper_execution_ledger/record.py:90-104` defines ledger lineage fields and `live_blocked`, but no paper-mode mode or flag timestamp field. This makes the ledger adjacent to paper mode rather than gated by paper mode.

### Risk gateway enforcement

BLOCKED.

`v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:25-29` accepts only an `OrchestratorDecisionRecord` and clock. Open-long and open-short decisions become `allow` at `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:49-54`, and `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:67-78` hard-codes `live_blocked=True` on the returned `RiskDecisionRecord`.

The composition boundary has the same contract: `v2/backend/app/composition/risk_gateway/runtime.py:15-25` builds an evaluator whose runtime input is only `decision`. Since no paper-mode flag is passed to the gateway, the gateway cannot deny when the flag is absent, invalid, stale, or not live-blocked.

The risk domain does enforce `live_blocked is True` once supplied: `v2/backend/app/domain/risk_gateway/record.py:56-81` includes the field and validates it. That is useful, but it is not equivalent to enforcing paper-mode composition because the service currently creates the value itself.

### Decision explainability

PARTIAL, blocked by the enforcement gap.

The API surfaces advertise explain endpoints, for example `/decisions/{decision_id}/explain`, `/risk-decisions/{risk_decision_id}/explain`, and `/paper-trades/{paper_trade_id}/explain`, but the inspected files are scaffold metadata rather than an operator-visible explanation pipeline. Test harnesses project paper-mode evidence in adjacent contexts, but the production service contracts for risk and ledger do not require that evidence and therefore cannot prove that explained records were produced through a valid paper-mode gate.

## Concrete blockers

1. Risk gateway does not require paper-mode evidence.
   - Evidence: `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:25-29`, `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:49-54`, `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:67-78`, and `v2/backend/app/composition/risk_gateway/runtime.py:15-25`.
   - Impact: an `open_long` or `open_short` decision can become risk `allow` without any valid `PaperModeFlag`.
   - Non-live autofix task: extend the risk gateway service and composition boundary with keyword-only `paper_mode_flag: PaperModeFlag`, reject non-flag inputs, and deny before any allow action when the flag is invalid, missing, stale by deterministic policy, or `live_blocked` is not true.

2. Paper ledger does not require or preserve paper-mode evidence.
   - Evidence: `v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:26-30`, `v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:80-92`, and `v2/backend/app/domain/paper_execution_ledger/record.py:90-104`.
   - Impact: a ledger entry can be produced from a risk record without recording the runtime mode or flag timestamp that made the path paper-only.
   - Non-live autofix task: extend paper ledger assembly/composition to require `PaperModeFlag`, then preserve `paper_mode` and `paper_mode_flag_emitted_ts_ms` either directly on `PaperExecutionLedgerEntry` or in a typed ledger evidence envelope.

3. End-to-end paper path is not composed as a gated contract.
   - Evidence: paper mode, risk gateway, and paper ledger each have independent composition roots, but no inspected production composition root wires `paper_mode -> risk_gateway -> paper_execution_ledger` with the flag as a mandatory dependency.
   - Impact: existing tests can prove local purity and adjacent evidence projection, but not that every paper ledger event came through a valid paper-mode gate.
   - Non-live autofix task: add a deterministic local-only contract harness covering paper mode, orchestrator decision, risk gateway, paper ledger, and explainability. Include negative cases for missing flag, invalid type, `live_blocked=False`, unsupported mode, and stale or mismatched flag timestamp.

4. Explainability does not close the enforcement loop.
   - Evidence: API explain endpoints are scaffold metadata, and ledger/risk records do not carry the paper-mode mode/timestamp needed to explain the runtime flag used at decision time.
   - Impact: operators cannot rely on explained risk or paper-ledger records to prove the decision was produced in paper mode rather than merely stamped with `live_blocked=True`.
   - Non-live autofix task: project paper-mode mode, emitted timestamp, and live-blocked posture into risk, ledger, replay, and explainability payloads, with tests asserting every risk-approved and ledger-visible row can explain the runtime mode used.

## Proposed non-live autofix tasks

- `paper_mode_risk_gateway_flag_required_autofix`: require `PaperModeFlag` at risk gateway service/composition boundaries and block approvals without valid flag evidence.
- `paper_mode_ledger_flag_lineage_autofix`: require and preserve `PaperModeFlag` evidence in ledger records or a typed ledger evidence envelope.
- `paper_mode_end_to_end_gated_contract_autofix`: add pure local contract tests for the paper-mode-to-risk-to-ledger path with positive and negative cases.
- `paper_mode_explainability_runtime_evidence_autofix`: include runtime mode, flag timestamp, and live-blocked posture in downstream explainability payloads.
