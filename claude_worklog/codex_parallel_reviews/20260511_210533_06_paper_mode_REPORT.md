## Concrete blockers

1. Risk gateway does not require paper-mode evidence.
   - Evidence: `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:25-29`, `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:49-60`, and `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:67-78`.
   - Impact: an `open_long` or `open_short` decision can become risk `allow` without any valid `PaperModeFlag`.
   - Non-live autofix task: extend the risk gateway service/composition boundary with keyword-only `paper_mode_flag: PaperModeFlag`, reject non-flag inputs, and deny before any allow action when flag evidence is missing, invalid, stale by deterministic policy, mismatched, or not `live_blocked=True`.

2. Paper ledger does not require or preserve paper-mode evidence.
   - Evidence: `v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:26-30`, `v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:80-92`, and `v2/backend/app/domain/paper_execution_ledger/record.py:90-104`.
   - Impact: a ledger entry can be produced from a risk record without recording the runtime mode or flag timestamp that made the path paper-only.
   - Non-live autofix task: extend paper ledger assembly/composition to require `PaperModeFlag`, then preserve `paper_mode` and `paper_mode_flag_emitted_ts_ms` either on `PaperExecutionLedgerEntry` or in a typed ledger evidence envelope.

3. No production paper-mode pipeline composes flag -> risk gateway -> paper ledger.
   - Evidence: `v2/backend/app/services/paper_loop.py:1` is a placeholder, while the relevant production pieces remain separate composition roots.
   - Impact: tests can prove local purity and projection behavior, but not that every paper ledger event passed through a valid paper-mode gate.
   - Non-live autofix task: add a deterministic local-only paper-mode pipeline harness that composes paper mode, orchestrator decision, risk gateway, paper ledger, and explainability, with negative cases for missing flag, invalid type, `live_blocked=False`, unsupported mode, stale timestamp, and mismatched lineage.

4. Explainability does not close the runtime evidence loop.
   - Evidence: `v2/backend/app/api/v1/paper.py:14-34` advertises explain route metadata only, and the risk/ledger records do not carry paper-mode mode/timestamp evidence.
   - Impact: an operator cannot prove from explained risk or ledger records that the decision was produced through paper mode rather than stamped with `live_blocked=True`.
   - Non-live autofix task: project runtime mode, flag timestamp, and live-blocked posture into risk, ledger, replay, and explainability payloads, with assertions that every risk-approved and ledger-visible row can explain the runtime mode used.
