## Verdict

BLOCKED.

The narrow paper-mode flag, paper ledger, risk gateway, and decision explainability surfaces are pure and non-live, but the MVP is not enforceably composed end to end. A valid `PaperModeFlag` is not a required input to risk approval or paper-ledger recording, and ledger records do not carry the paper-mode value/timestamp. Current harnesses prove adjacent evidence projections, not a gated path where paper mode is mandatory before risk or ledger actions.

### Paper ledger integration

### Risk gateway enforcement

BLOCKED.

`v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:25-79` accepts only an `OrchestratorDecisionRecord` plus clock. Open-long/open-short decisions become `allow` at `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:49-54`, and the output hard-codes `live_blocked=True` at `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:67-79`.

### Decision explainability

PARTIAL, blocked by enforcement gap.

- `v2/backend/tests/unit/decision_explainability_data_contract/harness.py:40-80` creates one `PaperModeFlag` and projects `paper_mode_live_blocked` plus `paper_mode_mode` into every explainability envelope.

The operator-visible explainability tests are useful, but they do not prove the risk and ledger records being explained were produced only after valid paper-mode gating.

## Concrete Blockers

1. Risk gateway does not require paper-mode evidence.
   - Non-live autofix: extend `assemble_risk_decision_record` and `build_risk_decision_evaluator` with a keyword-only `paper_mode_flag: PaperModeFlag`.
   - Reject or deny before `allow` when the flag is missing, not a `PaperModeFlag`, has an unsupported mode, has `live_blocked != True`, or is stale by an explicit deterministic policy.
   - Add unit tests proving open-long/open-short cannot become `allow` without valid paper-mode evidence.

2. Paper ledger does not require or record paper-mode evidence.
   - Non-live autofix: extend ledger assembly/composition to require `paper_mode_flag: PaperModeFlag`.
   - Propagate `paper_mode` and `paper_mode_flag_emitted_ts_ms` into `PaperExecutionLedgerEntry` or a typed paper-ledger evidence envelope.
   - Add tests proving ledger recording rejects missing/invalid paper-mode evidence and preserves mode/timestamp on every recorded row.

3. End-to-end paper path is only harness-adjacent, not enforced.
   - Non-live autofix: add a deterministic local-only integration harness composing `paper_mode -> orchestrator_decision -> risk_gateway -> paper_execution_ledger -> replay/explainability`.
   - Include negative cases for missing flag, `live_blocked=False`, unrecognized mode, and stale/mismatched flag timestamp.
   - Keep it pure: no Redis, no exchange adapters, no live services, no HTTP, no file persistence.

4. Explainability is missing ledger-level paper-mode evidence.
   - Non-live autofix: include paper-mode mode and flag timestamp in ledger/replay/explainability payloads, not only in the standalone data-contract harness.
   - Add tests proving every risk-approved and paper-ledger-visible row can explain the runtime mode and live-blocked posture used at decision time.

## Proposed Non-Live Autofix Tasks

- `paper_mode_risk_gateway_flag_required_autofix`: require `PaperModeFlag` at the risk gateway service and composition boundary, with approval-blocking tests for missing or invalid paper-mode evidence.
- `paper_mode_ledger_flag_lineage_autofix`: require and preserve paper-mode evidence in paper ledger records or a typed ledger evidence envelope.
- `paper_mode_end_to_end_gated_contract_autofix`: add a deterministic local-only contract test across paper mode, orchestrator decision, risk gateway, paper ledger, replay, and explainability.
- `paper_mode_explainability_runtime_evidence_autofix`: project paper mode, flag timestamp, and live-blocked posture into ledger/replay/explainability evidence.
