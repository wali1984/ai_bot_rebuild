## Verdict

CODEX_PARALLEL_REVIEW_BLOCKED

The implementation has a clean non-live paper-mode flag slice, plus non-live risk and paper-ledger record assemblers. It is blocked for Paper Mode MVP readiness because the paper-mode flag is not required by, passed into, or enforced by the risk gateway or paper ledger path. The current downstream records set `live_blocked=True` internally, which proves those individual records are live-blocked, but does not prove that a composed paper-mode runtime flag controls the decision chain.

## Checks

### Paper mode flag composition

Pass with integration gap.

Evidence:
- `v2/backend/app/domain/paper_mode/flag.py` defines `PaperModeFlag` with allowed modes `paper` and `live_blocked`, non-negative timestamp validation, and `live_blocked is True` enforcement.
- `v2/backend/app/services/paper_mode/service.py` only accepts requested modes `paper` and `live_blocked`, rejects live/unknown modes, validates the injected clock, and returns `PaperModeFlag(..., live_blocked=True)`.
- `v2/backend/app/composition/paper_mode/runtime.py:21-39` composes a `PaperModeRuntime` with `paper_mode_now(requested_mode=...)`.

Gap:
- `PaperModeRuntime` is standalone. I did not find an app/composition root that threads its `PaperModeFlag` into orchestrator decision, risk gateway, paper ledger, or explainability production code.

### No live execution

Pass for reviewed Paper Mode MVP paths.

- No exchange adapter invocation, order placement, Redis write, persistence layer, scheduler, or service restart path appears in these reviewed slices.

- `v2/backend/app/composition/paper_execution_ledger/runtime.py` returns a callable wrapper around paper ledger record assembly only.

### Paper ledger integration

Blocked as MVP integration.

Evidence:
- `v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:26-93` accepts only a `RiskDecisionRecord` and `now_ms_clock`, maps risk reason codes to mirror ledger action/reason codes, and returns `PaperExecutionLedgerEntry(..., live_blocked=True)`.
- This is good non-live ledger mirroring, but it does not require a `PaperModeFlag`, does not check `paper_mode_flag.mode`, and does not carry paper-mode evidence into the ledger entry.

Blocker:
- A caller can assemble a paper ledger entry from any valid `RiskDecisionRecord` without proving that the paper-mode runtime flag was emitted for the same decision cycle.

### Risk gateway enforcement

Blocked as MVP integration.

Evidence:
- `v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:25-79` accepts only an `OrchestratorDecisionRecord` and `now_ms_clock`.
- `v2/backend/app/composition/risk_gateway/runtime.py:15-27` builds an evaluator that accepts only `decision`.
- The risk service emits allow for `open_long`/`open_short` and deny for `hold`/`abstain`, then sets `live_blocked=True` internally.

Blocker:
- The risk gateway does not require a `PaperModeFlag` and cannot deny or fail closed when paper-mode evidence is missing, stale, malformed, or not `paper`/`live_blocked`. Since the signature has no paper-mode input, tests cannot prove "no risk approval without paper-mode flag".

### Decision explainability

Partial, blocked for app integration.

Evidence:
- `v2/backend/tests/unit/decision_explainability_data_contract/harness.py:40-81` creates a paper-mode flag and projects `paper_mode_live_blocked` plus `paper_mode_mode` into a test harness envelope.
- Paper ledger and replay/backtest explainability harnesses carry `live_blocked` and lineage fields.

Blocker:
- The paper-mode explainability projection is currently a unit-test harness, not a production/app composition contract. It constructs `paper_mode_flag` separately from risk and ledger records, so it does not prove that the same paper-mode evidence enforced risk approval and ledger recording.

   - Fail closed before `allow` when the flag is absent/not a `PaperModeFlag`.

   - Require the same `PaperModeFlag` or a risk record that explicitly carries paper-mode fields.
   - Carry `paper_mode_mode` and `paper_mode_live_blocked` into ledger/explainability evidence, or add an adjacent immutable evidence record if changing `PaperExecutionLedgerEntry` is too broad.
   - Add tests proving ledger recording fails without paper-mode evidence.

   - Assert no exchange/Redis/FastAPI imports and no live execution surfaces.
