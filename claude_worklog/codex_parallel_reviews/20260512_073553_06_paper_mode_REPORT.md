## Passing observations

- Paper mode flag composition is strict at the narrow component boundary. `PaperModeFlag` permits only `paper` and `live_blocked` modes and requires `live_blocked is True` (`v2/backend/app/domain/paper_mode/flag.py:8`, `v2/backend/app/domain/paper_mode/flag.py:46`). The service rejects unknown requested modes and always returns a live-blocked flag (`v2/backend/app/services/paper_mode/service.py:13`, `v2/backend/app/services/paper_mode/service.py:47`).
- The inspected paper-mode, risk-gateway, and paper-ledger domain/service/composition modules contain no exchange, order, cancel, leverage, margin, Redis, FastAPI, or live-enabled execution branch matches in the targeted scan.
- Risk records are live-blocked by construction in the service (`v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:67`) and enforced in the domain record (`v2/backend/app/domain/risk_gateway/record.py:68`, `v2/backend/app/domain/risk_gateway/record.py:81`).
- Paper ledger entries mirror risk decisions into record-allow or record-deny ledger rows (`v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:59`) and enforce `live_blocked is True` in the domain (`v2/backend/app/domain/paper_execution_ledger/record.py:103`, `v2/backend/app/domain/paper_execution_ledger/record.py:151`).

## Blockers

### BLOCKER 1: paper-mode flag is not required by the risk gateway

`assemble_risk_decision_record` accepts only an `OrchestratorDecisionRecord` and a clock (`v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:25`). It does not accept `PaperModeFlag`, does not validate that a fresh paper-mode flag exists, and does not copy the paper-mode mode or flag timestamp into the risk decision.

This means risk decisions can be assembled in the current V2 surface without proving that the paper-mode runtime flag participated in the decision. The resulting record is live-blocked, but the live-blocked value is hard-coded inside the risk service (`v2/backend/app/services/risk_gateway/service.py:78`) rather than enforced as a handoff from the paper-mode flag.

### BLOCKER 2: ledger integration mirrors risk decisions but not paper-mode evidence

`assemble_paper_execution_ledger_entry` accepts only a `RiskDecisionRecord` and a clock (`v2/backend/app/services/paper_execution_ledger/service.py:26`). The ledger entry records risk lineage and mirrored risk action/reason values, but it has no fields for paper mode, paper flag timestamp, or paper-mode flag ID/evidence.

The paper-mode evidence harness creates a paper-mode flag and separately replays prebuilt ledger fixtures (`v2/backend/tests/unit/paper_mode_evidence_collection_harness/harness.py:30`). It asserts the flag is live-blocked (`v2/backend/tests/unit/paper_mode_evidence_collection_harness/harness.py:40`) but does not prove that the same flag was required to create the risk decision or ledger entry being replayed.

### BLOCKER 3: risk gateway enforcement is fail-closed for records, but not for missing paper-mode input

The risk gateway domain rejects `live_blocked=False`, and the service maps open-long/open-short to allow records while still setting `live_blocked=True`. That is useful, but it is not enough for Paper Mode MVP enforcement because missing paper-mode input is not an error path. There is no test or source path proving that a risk-allowed paper decision is rejected when the paper-mode flag is absent, stale, unrecognized, or not live-blocked.

### BLOCKER 4: decision explainability does not include paper-mode evidence

The inspected explainability projection for orchestrator decisions includes decision action, decision reason, prediction inputs, worker health, and `live_blocked` (`v2/backend/tests/unit/decision_explainability_orchestrator_decision_projection/harness.py:17`). It does not include paper mode, paper flag emitted timestamp, risk decision ID/action/reason, paper trade ID, ledger action/reason, or paper ledger timestamp.

As a result, a reviewed decision can explain why the orchestrator chose open/hold/abstain, but cannot explain whether that decision was made under paper mode, whether the risk gateway saw the paper-mode flag, or which paper ledger row captured the mirrored outcome.

## Proposed non-live autofix tasks

1. Add a non-live paper-mode risk handoff contract that requires `PaperModeFlag` as an input to the risk gateway composition/service path. Reject missing, non-record, stale, non-paper/non-live-blocked, or `live_blocked=False` flags before a `RiskDecisionRecord` can be emitted.

2. Extend risk decision evidence with paper-mode fields such as `paper_mode`, `paper_mode_flag_emitted_ts_ms`, and an explicit paper-mode reason/evidence code. Keep the existing `live_blocked=True` invariant and avoid any exchange, Redis, FastAPI, background loop, or order API.

3. Extend the paper ledger assembler to consume the risk decision that already contains paper-mode evidence, and propagate those paper-mode fields into `PaperExecutionLedgerEntry` or a companion non-live evidence envelope. Add tests proving every allow and deny ledger entry carries the same paper-mode evidence as its risk decision.

4. Add an end-to-end offline harness for `PaperModeFlag -> OrchestratorDecisionRecord -> RiskDecisionRecord -> PaperExecutionLedgerEntry -> explainability envelope`. Cover open long, open short, hold, abstain, default deny, missing flag, stale flag, and invalid flag cases.

5. Add decision explainability projection rows that include paper mode, flag timestamp, risk action/reason, ledger action/reason, and all lineage IDs. Assert that explainability cannot silently default to live or omit paper-mode evidence.

6. Keep all autofixes pure and local: deterministic clocks, in-memory objects, no Redis writes, no exchange clients, no live service restarts, no order placement, no leverage/margin changes, and no deployment.

## Verdict

The inspected code is safe as narrow non-live component work, but it is not ready as a Paper Mode MVP. The paper-mode flag is not yet a required input to risk evaluation, ledger rows do not carry paper-mode evidence, and decision explainability cannot prove that a risk/ledger outcome occurred under paper mode.
