# Phase 2W — Next Consolidated Milestone Recommendation

## Chosen milestone
**2X_EXTERNAL_MANUAL_POSITION_QUARANTINE** is recommended as the single next consolidated non-live milestone after `V2_BACKTEST_AND_PAPER_MVP_READY` and after Phase 2W.

## Rationale (anchored to on-disk legacy evidence)

Three independent on-disk evidence pointers converge on the external/manual position quarantine gap as the next item the non-live build chain must close:

1. **REQ_0013 phase order anchor.** `claude_worklog/requirements_inbox/REQ_0013_SMC_LIQUIDITY_SHADOW_FEATURES.md` lines 9–22 explicitly orders the six prerequisites; line 15 lists "external/manual position quarantine" as the first item, ahead of provenance/dedupe/attribution (line 16) and degraded-state fail-closed gates (line 17). The requirement's own placement-in-roadmap block at `claude_worklog/requirements_inbox/REQ_0013_SMC_LIQUIDITY_SHADOW_FEATURES.md` lines 192–199 reaffirms quarantine at the bottom of the required-order stack. Opening 2X first matches the literal order in REQ_0013 rather than reshuffling it.

2. **REQ_0013 manual-position safety rule.** `claude_worklog/requirements_inbox/REQ_0013_SMC_LIQUIDITY_SHADOW_FEATURES.md` line 31 states "do not use SMC features to justify DCA, hedging, rescue trades, or risk-adds on manual/external positions." That prohibition cannot be enforced without a typed `ManualPositionFlag` / `ExternalPositionQuarantineRecord` value object that downstream services can pattern-match on. The risk gateway default-deny base layer at `PHASE2G_C_RISK_GATEWAY_COMPOSITION_ROOT_CODEX_PASS` (file `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/risk_gateway_impl/25_2G_C_RISK_GATEWAY_COMPOSITION_ROOT_CODEX_GO_NO_GO.md`) cannot satisfy this prohibition by itself because its current reason taxonomy at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/risk_gateway_impl/01_PHASE_2G_LEGACY_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md` lines 30–40 contains no manual-position or external-position member.

3. **REQ_0022 LAB hedge-unwind tie-in.** The LAB hedge-unwind / short-squeeze failure case at `claude_worklog/legacy_readonly_audit/08_FAILURE_CASE_REGISTER.md` lines 7–27 (referencing `claude_worklog/legacy_failure_cases/2026_05_LAB_HEDGE_UNWIND_SHORT_SQUEEZE_FAILURE.md`) is the single most-documented legacy-loss case. It is also one in which a hedged position with manual / external semantics ended in a naked directional exposure. The replay-case fixture at `PHASE2M_REPLAY_CASE_LAB_HEDGE_UNWIND_LEGACY_FAILURE_EVIDENCE_READY` (`claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/replay_case_lab_hedge_unwind/01_LEGACY_FAILURE_EVIDENCE.md` line 46) and its trainer-parity scenario row at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/trainer_lineage_parity_fields_extension/02_PHASE_2V_TRAINER_LINEAGE_PARITY_FIELDS_SPEC.md` line 21 give 2X a ready-made first regression input. The consolidated failure-signals table at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/legacy_evidence/02_CURRENT_LEGACY_FAILURE_SIGNALS.md` line 25 already classifies "External / manual position quarantine missing" as RISK_GATEWAY_DEFAULT_DENY_MVP follow-up + REQ_0013 phase-1 quarantine.

## Legacy failure each candidate addresses
- **2X_EXTERNAL_MANUAL_POSITION_QUARANTINE** addresses LAB hedge-unwind / squeeze residual exposure (REQ_0022 — `claude_worklog/legacy_readonly_audit/08_FAILURE_CASE_REGISTER.md` lines 7–27 and `claude_worklog/requirements_inbox/REQ_0022_LEGACY_FAILURE_HEDGE_UNWIND_AND_SQUEEZE_RISK.md` lines 26–47) by gating any hedge-close action against a typed quarantine flag, and addresses the manual-position SMC misuse failure class (REQ_0013 line 31) by giving downstream SMC-shadow consumers a typed flag they must respect.
- **2Y_PROVENANCE_DEDUPE_ATTRIBUTION** addresses feature/source provenance ambiguity and double-counting (REQ_0013 prerequisite 2; orchestrator stale/duplicate-signal handling row at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/legacy_evidence/01_BUILD_IMPACT_MAP.md` lines 28–34).
- **2Z_DEGRADED_STATE_FAIL_CLOSED_GATES** addresses stale-data invisibility and degraded-source action (REQ_0013 lines 121–129 SMC/liquidity/OI/orderbook freshness gates and REQ_0013 lines 133–137 required-behavior block).

- **2Y_PROVENANCE_DEDUPE_ATTRIBUTION** deferred to second position. Reasoning: lineage IDs `feature_snapshot_id`, `prediction_id`, `decision_id`, `risk_decision_id` are already present and certified per `claude_worklog/v2_requirements/03_PREDICTION_SIGNAL_DECISION_ID_CHAIN.md`, and trainer parity fields (`model_version`, `checkpoint_id`, `confidence_raw`, `confidence_calibrated`, `trainer_worker_liveness`) are added by `PHASE2V_TRAINER_LINEAGE_PARITY_FIELDS_EXTENSION_CODEX_PASS` (`claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/trainer_lineage_parity_fields_extension/11_CODEX_REREVIEW_AFTER_VENV_PYTEST_GO_NO_GO.md`). The duplicate-signal scenario row at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/trainer_lineage_parity_fields_extension/02_PHASE_2V_TRAINER_LINEAGE_PARITY_FIELDS_SPEC.md` line 19 (`duplicate_signal_blocked`) and the orchestrator stale-signal row at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/legacy_evidence/01_BUILD_IMPACT_MAP.md` line 31 already exist as evidence anchors. The remaining work is incremental hardening (typed `ProvenanceRecord` / `DedupeDecisionRecord` value objects), not a foundational missing piece, so 2Y is safer to open after 2X has gated the external-position attack surface.
- **2Z_DEGRADED_STATE_FAIL_CLOSED_GATES** deferred to third position. Reasoning: the orchestrator-side abstain reason codes (`abstain_freshness_stale`, `abstain_freshness_missing`, `abstain_worker_degraded`, `abstain_worker_critical`, `abstain_worker_unknown` at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/orchestrator_decision_impl/01_PHASE_2F_LEGACY_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md` lines 31–37) and the risk-side default-deny base layer (`claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/risk_gateway_impl/01_PHASE_2G_LEGACY_EVIDENCE_REVIEW.md` lines 30–40) already give a fail-closed posture for trainer-side degraded states, and the trainer worker liveness taxonomy (`alive`/`degraded`/`worker_dead` per `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/trainer_lineage_parity_fields_extension/02_PHASE_2V_TRAINER_LINEAGE_PARITY_FIELDS_SPEC.md` line 11) is already typed and PASS. The remaining 2Z work is consolidation of source-level degraded states (SMC freshness, liquidation freshness, OI freshness, orderbook freshness per REQ_0013 lines 121–129) into a single typed record. That consolidation depends on having a quarantine flag (2X) and a provenance contract (2Y) so the degraded-state record can carry per-source provenance pointers. 2Z therefore opens last.

## Typed-contract scope of the recommended milestone
The 2X scope is bounded as follows. No execution-side surface, no new lineage ID, no live-gate flip — only typed contract + non-live unit tests:

- **Typed value objects only**: `ManualPositionFlag` (typed flag with two state constants `MANUAL_POSITION_QUARANTINED` and `MANUAL_POSITION_NOT_PRESENT`, both carrying `live_blocked is True`); `ExternalPositionQuarantineRecord` (typed mirror over `RiskDecisionRecord` carrying the per-row quarantine flag, `risk_decision_id`, `decision_id`, `prediction_id`, `feature_snapshot_id`, and the existing five Phase 2V trainer-parity fields without introducing a new lineage ID).
- **Service layer**: `assemble_external_position_quarantine_record` pure function that takes the upstream `RiskDecisionRecord` plus a `ManualPositionFlag` and returns the typed mirror record.
- **Composition root**: `build_external_position_quarantine_runtime(now_ms_clock=...)` factory mirroring the Phase 2F/2G/2H/2I/2J/2K composition-root pattern.
- **Non-live unit tests only**: `test_external_position_quarantine_record_construction.py`, `test_external_position_quarantine_assembler.py`, `test_external_position_quarantine_composition_root.py`, plus a regression fixture row that consumes the existing LAB scenario at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/replay_case_lab_hedge_unwind/01_LEGACY_FAILURE_EVIDENCE.md` and the existing trainer-parity row at `claude_worklog/phase2_core_rebuild/trainer_lineage_parity_fields_extension/02_PHASE_2V_TRAINER_LINEAGE_PARITY_FIELDS_SPEC.md` line 20 (`hedge_close_residual_exposure_blocked`).
- **No execution-side surface**: no paper trader, no shadow trader, no live trader, no replay engine, no scheduler, no background loop, no FastAPI surface, no Redis adapter, no GPU runner, no model-loading subsystem, no strategy library.
- **No new lineage ID**: the `risk_decision_id`, `decision_id`, `prediction_id`, `feature_snapshot_id` already at `claude_worklog/v2_requirements/03_PREDICTION_SIGNAL_DECISION_ID_CHAIN.md` and the five Phase 2V trainer-parity fields are mirrored only.
- **No live-gate flip**: `FINAL_NON_LIVE_REBUILD_READY_FOR_LIVE_GATE_REVIEW` remains blocked and human-only; `live_blocked == True` invariant enforced at the value-object layer of the new typed surface.
